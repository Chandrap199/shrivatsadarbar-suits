Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our handcrafted luxury collection

1. Are your Banarasi sarees and readymade suits authentic and handcrafted?
Yes. Every creation at Shri Vatsa Darbar is authentically crafted by master artisans in Varanasi, utilizing genuine zari, pure katan silk, and premium georgette and chiffon fabrics.
2. What sizing options are available for your readymade suits?
Our designer suits are precision-tailored in readymade standard sizes ranging from Small (S) to Double XL (XXL). Each product page features a detailed size guide to ensure a flawless fit.
3. Do you provide custom tailoring or made-to-order services?
We specialize exclusively in ready-to-wear designer suits and handcrafted sarees to maintain our rigorous luxury finish. Custom tailoring services are not offered at this time.
4. How should I care for my Banarasi silk sarees and delicate georgette/chiffon suits?
Due to the delicate nature of pure silk, zari work, and fine chiffon/georgette weaves, we strictly recommend dry clean only. Store your pieces wrapped in muslin cloth in a dry, cool place away from direct sunlight.
5. What is the delivery timeline for orders across India?
Standard dispatch from our Varanasi atelier takes 2–4 business days, and delivery across India typically takes 4–7 business days depending on your destination.
6. How does the WhatsApp Inquiry and Checkout process work?
You can browse our masterclass collections, add your preferred items to your Inquiry Cart, and click "Proceed Inquiry via WhatsApp". This instantly shares your exact item selection with our concierge team for immediate order confirmation and shipping updates.
7. Is an unboxing video required in case of a return or transit issue?
Yes. To ensure a transparent resolution for any rare transit damages or incorrect items, we require a continuous, unedited unboxing video starting from the sealed outer package. Please review our Return & Refund policy for full instructions.
