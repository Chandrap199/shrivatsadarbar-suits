Cancellation Policy

Home Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: 27 August 2026

At ShriVatsaDarbar, we understand that circumstances can change after an order is placed. This Cancellation Policy explains when an order may be cancelled, when a cancellation request may be accepted, and when an eligible refund may be issued.

01

When Can an Order Be Cancelled?

A cancellation request should be made as soon as possible after placing an order.

If the order has not yet entered processing, the customer may request cancellation.

Once an order has entered processing, been customised, specially prepared, packed or dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible under this policy.

02

Cancellation Eligibility

A cancellation with a refund is eligible only when the cancellation request is received and accepted before the order enters processing.

To be eligible for cancellation:

  • The order must not have entered processing.
  • The product must not have been customised or specially prepared.
  • The product must not have been packed for dispatch.
  • The order must not have already been dispatched.
Important A cancellation request is not automatically accepted merely because it has been submitted. Our team must first confirm the current processing status of the order.
03

Change of Mind

Customers may sometimes request cancellation because they have changed their mind.

Examples include:

  • “I don't want the product anymore.”
  • “I changed my mind.”
  • “I don't like the product anymore.”
  • “I ordered by mistake.”

If such a request is received before processing begins, ShriVatsaDarbar may accept the cancellation and process the applicable refund.

If processing has already started, the order may not be cancelled merely because the customer has changed their mind.

04

Cancellation After Processing

Once an order has entered processing, our team may have committed time, materials and operational resources to preparing the product.

For this reason, a cancellation request received after processing has started may not be accepted.

This includes orders that have already been customised, specially prepared, packed, labelled or handed over for dispatch.

Processing status matters. The availability of cancellation is determined by the actual status of the order when the cancellation request is received.
05

Cancellation After Dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched and handed over to the courier or delivery partner, it cannot normally be cancelled through this Cancellation Policy.

Customers should not refuse delivery solely as a substitute for following the applicable return or refund procedure.

If there is an issue with the product after delivery, including a defect, damage, incorrect product or material difference from the agreed description, please contact us and refer to our Return & Refund Policy .

06

Refund for an Accepted Cancellation

Where ShriVatsaDarbar accepts an eligible cancellation before processing begins and a refund is due, the eligible amount will normally be returned to the original payment source, subject to the payment method and applicable processing timelines.

A refund will not be created merely because a customer requests cancellation after the order has entered processing.

Refund Method Where a cancellation is approved and a refund is applicable, ShriVatsaDarbar will normally use the original payment source or another lawful refund mechanism appropriate to the transaction.
07

How to Request Cancellation

Customers should contact ShriVatsaDarbar as soon as possible after placing the order.

Cancellation requests may be submitted through WhatsApp or the contact details provided on our website.

Please provide the order details necessary for us to identify the order and assess whether processing has started.

  • Customer name
  • Order number, if available
  • Product name
  • Phone number used for the order
  • Reason for cancellation
08

Cancellation by ShriVatsaDarbar

In exceptional circumstances, ShriVatsaDarbar may need to cancel an order.

This may include circumstances such as:

  • Product unavailability
  • Inability to fulfil the order
  • Incorrect or unavailable delivery information
  • Operational or courier limitations
  • Circumstances beyond our reasonable control

Where an amount has already been paid and a refund is applicable, we will communicate the cancellation and process the applicable refund.

09

Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Cancellation Policy is intended to exclude or restrict any consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted under applicable Indian law.

If a product is defective, deficient, spurious, materially different from its advertised or agreed characteristics, or another legally recognised consumer remedy applies, the applicable legal requirements and our relevant customer policy will apply.

Your statutory consumer rights remain protected. This Cancellation Policy explains voluntary cancellation of an order. It does not remove rights or remedies that are required to remain available under applicable law.
