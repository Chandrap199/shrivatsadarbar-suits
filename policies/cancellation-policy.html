Cancellation Policy

ORDER CANCELLATION

Simple, Clear & Transparent

At ShriVatsaDarbar, we understand that circumstances can change after placing an order. Our cancellation policy explains when an order may be cancelled and when a refund may be issued.

01

When Can an Order Be Cancelled?

A cancellation request should be made as soon as possible after placing an order.

If the order has not yet entered processing, the customer may request cancellation.

Once an order has entered processing, been customised, prepared, packed or dispatched, cancellation may no longer be available under this Cancellation Policy.

02

Cancellation Eligibility

A cancellation with refund is eligible only when the cancellation request is received and accepted before the order enters processing.

  • The order must not have entered processing.
  • The product must not have been customised or specially prepared.
  • The order must not have been packed for dispatch.
  • The order must not have already been dispatched.
Important: A customer simply changing their mind after the order has entered processing does not create an automatic cancellation or refund entitlement under this policy.
03

Change of Mind

Cancellation requests based only on a change of mind are subject to the processing status of the order.

Examples include:

  • "I don't want the product anymore."
  • "I changed my mind."
  • "I don't like the product anymore."
  • "I ordered by mistake."

If such a request is received before processing begins, ShriVatsaDarbar may accept the cancellation and process the applicable refund.

If processing has already started, the order may not be cancelled merely because the customer has changed their mind.

04

Orders That Are Already Processed

Once an order has entered processing, cancellation may no longer be possible.

This includes orders that have been customised, specially prepared, packed or otherwise committed for dispatch.

If the order has already been dispatched, cancellation is no longer available under this policy.

Where the customer has an issue with the product after delivery, the applicable Return & Refund Policy should be followed.

05

Full Refund for Eligible Cancellation

Where ShriVatsaDarbar accepts a cancellation before processing begins, the customer will be eligible for a refund of the amount actually paid for the cancelled order.

The refund will be processed back to the original payment source used for the order.

The time taken for the amount to appear in the customer's account may depend on the payment provider, bank or financial institution involved.

06

Cancellation After Dispatch

Once an order has been dispatched, cancellation is no longer available under this Cancellation Policy.

If the delivered product is damaged, defective, incorrect, deficient or materially different from what was advertised or agreed, the customer may contact ShriVatsaDarbar and use the applicable Return & Refund process.

Nothing in this policy is intended to restrict or exclude any mandatory consumer right or remedy available under applicable law.

07

How to Request Cancellation

To request cancellation, please contact ShriVatsaDarbar as soon as possible after placing your order.

  • Provide your order number.
  • Provide the name or details of the product ordered.
  • Clearly state that you wish to cancel the order.
  • Contact us before the order enters processing wherever possible.

Our team will check the current status of the order and confirm whether cancellation is possible.

08

Cancellation Confirmation

A cancellation request is not considered complete until ShriVatsaDarbar confirms that the order has been successfully cancelled.

Customers should retain the cancellation confirmation and order reference until the refund, where applicable, has been completed.

09

Consumer Rights & Legal Protection

This Cancellation Policy is intended to provide clear commercial terms for order cancellations.

It does not restrict or exclude any consumer rights or remedies that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable Indian law.

Where a product is defective, deficient, spurious, incorrect, materially different from the advertised or agreed characteristics, or otherwise covered by a mandatory legal remedy, the applicable consumer protection requirements will prevail.
