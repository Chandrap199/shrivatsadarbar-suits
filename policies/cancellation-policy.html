ORDER CANCELLATION

Simple, Clear & Transparent

Home > Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: 26 August 2026

At ShriVatsaDarbar, we understand that circumstances can change after placing an order. This Cancellation Policy explains when an order may be cancelled, when a cancellation request may be accepted, and when a refund may be applicable.

01

When Can an Order Be Cancelled?

A cancellation request should be made as soon as possible after placing an order.

If the order has not yet entered processing, the customer may request cancellation.

Once an order has entered processing, been customised, specially prepared, packed or dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible under this policy.

02

Cancellation Eligibility

A cancellation with refund is eligible only when the cancellation request is received and accepted before the order enters processing.

  • The order must not have entered processing.
  • The product must not have been customised or specially prepared.
  • The product must not have been packed for dispatch.
  • The order must not have already been dispatched.
Important A customer simply changing their mind after the order has entered processing does not automatically create a right to cancellation or refund under this policy.
03

Change of Mind

Cancellation requests based only on a change of mind are subject to the processing status of the order.

Examples may include:

  • “I don't want the product anymore.”
  • “I changed my mind.”
  • “I don't like the product anymore.”
  • “I ordered by mistake.”

If such a request is received before processing begins, ShriVatsaDarbar may accept the cancellation and process the applicable refund.

If processing has already started, the order may not be cancelled merely because the customer has changed their mind.

04

Cancellation After Processing

Once an order has entered processing, our team may have committed time, materials and operational resources to preparing the product.

For this reason, cancellation after processing has started may not be accepted.

This includes orders that have already been customised, specially prepared, packed or handed over for dispatch.

05

Refund for an Accepted Cancellation

Where ShriVatsaDarbar accepts a cancellation before processing begins and a refund is due, the eligible amount will be refunded to the original payment source, subject to the payment method and applicable processing timelines.

Refunds will not be created merely because a customer requests cancellation after the order has entered processing.

Refund Method Where a refund is approved, ShriVatsaDarbar will normally use the original payment source or another lawful refund mechanism appropriate to the transaction.
06

How to Request Cancellation

Customers should contact ShriVatsaDarbar as soon as possible after placing the order.

Cancellation requests may be submitted through WhatsApp or the contact details provided on our website.

Please provide the order details necessary for us to identify the order and assess whether processing has started.

  • Customer name
  • Order number, if available
  • Product name
  • Phone number used for the order
  • Reason for cancellation
07

Cancellation by ShriVatsaDarbar

In exceptional circumstances, ShriVatsaDarbar may need to cancel an order, including situations involving product unavailability, an inability to fulfil the order, pricing or listing errors, or circumstances beyond our reasonable control.

Where an amount has already been paid and a refund is applicable, we will communicate the cancellation and initiate the applicable refund process.

08

Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Cancellation Policy is intended to exclude or restrict any consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable Indian law.

If a product is defective, materially different from its advertised description, damaged in transit, or otherwise gives rise to a statutory consumer remedy, the applicable Return, Refund or Exchange provisions and applicable law will apply.

09

Policy Changes

ShriVatsaDarbar may update this Cancellation Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our business practices, services or applicable legal requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page with the revised date.
