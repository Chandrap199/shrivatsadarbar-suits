Return & Refund Policy

We want you to love every piece you receive. If something isn't right, we're here to help make the experience right.

Last Updated: 26 August 2026

At ShriVatsaDarbar, we carefully select sarees and suits with an emphasis on quality, presentation and customer satisfaction.

This Return & Refund Policy explains when a product may be returned, how to request a return, how refunds are handled, and what conditions apply to returned products.
01

Return Eligibility

We accept return requests for products that are defective, damaged, incorrect, materially different from the product represented on our website, or otherwise eligible under this policy.

For eligible issues, please contact us as soon as reasonably possible after delivery so that we can review the issue and assist you.

02

How to Request a Return

To request a return, please contact ShriVatsaDarbar within 2 days from the date of delivery through WhatsApp or email.

  • Share your order number.
  • Mention the reason for the return.
  • Share clear photographs of the product and packaging.
  • Where useful, provide a short video showing the issue.
  • Do not send the product back before receiving return instructions from our team.
2-Day Return Window: For ordinary eligible returns, the return request must be raised within 2 days of delivery.
03

Product Condition

Except where a product is defective, damaged, incorrect or materially different from what was represented, returned products should be unused, unwashed and in their original condition.

Products should be returned with original tags, packaging and accessories wherever applicable.

Products that show signs of wear, washing, alteration, staining, damage after delivery or other customer-caused use may not be eligible for a discretionary return.

04

Review & Verification

Once we receive your return request, our team may review the photographs, videos, order details and condition of the product before approving the return.

Where a physical return is required, we will provide the applicable return instructions after reviewing the request.

05

Refunds

Where a refund is approved, the refund will normally be processed after the returned product has been received and inspected, where a physical return is required.

The refund method and processing timeline may depend on the original payment method and the applicable payment service provider.

We will communicate the applicable refund status and any relevant information to the customer.

06

Replacement & Exchange

Where appropriate, ShriVatsaDarbar may offer a replacement or exchange for an eligible product, subject to product availability.

If the same product is unavailable, our team will discuss the available resolution with you.

07

Return Shipping

Where the return is due to a defective, damaged, incorrect or materially misrepresented product, ShriVatsaDarbar will review the appropriate return-shipping arrangement with the customer.

For discretionary returns or exchanges that are offered under our commercial policy, any applicable return-shipping responsibility will be communicated before the return is arranged.

08

Non-Returnable Situations

Certain products or situations may not qualify for a discretionary return, including products that have been used, washed, altered or damaged after delivery.

This does not limit any consumer rights applicable where a product is defective, deficient, spurious, materially different from what was advertised or agreed, or delivered late where the applicable legal protections require a return or refund.

09

Refund Processing

Once a refund is approved, the processing time may vary according to the original payment method, banking system or payment service provider.

If a refund has been approved by ShriVatsaDarbar but has not appeared within the expected processing period, please contact us so that we can assist with the status.

10

Customer Support & Grievance

If you have a question, concern or complaint relating to an order, return or refund, please contact ShriVatsaDarbar using the contact details provided below.

We aim to acknowledge customer complaints promptly and work toward resolving eligible grievances within the applicable legal framework.

Important: Nothing in this policy is intended to exclude, restrict or waive any consumer right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded or restricted under applicable Indian law.
