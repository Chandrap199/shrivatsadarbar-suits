A Small Fit Tip

If your measurements fall between two sizes, consider your preferred fit. Choose the larger size for a more relaxed feel and the smaller size when you prefer a closer fit, subject to the construction and cut of the individual garment.

Sarees

Saree Fit Guide

Sarees are generally flexible in sizing because the drape can be adjusted to your preferred fit and styling.

Saree Length

A standard saree is commonly around 5.5 metres in length, with the blouse piece generally supplied separately within the saree fabric where applicable.

Blouse Fit

The blouse is the part where your personal measurements matter most. Measure your bust, underbust and shoulder area carefully before selecting or tailoring your blouse.

Drape Comfort

Your saree can be styled with a looser or more structured drape depending on your comfort, occasion and preferred look.

Your Personal Fit

Height, body proportions and the way you prefer to drape your saree can all influence how the finished look feels.

Important Saree Note

Saree sizing is different from ready-to-wear garments. The saree itself is generally not selected by S, M, L or XL. The blouse, when included, is the part that should be checked against the relevant product information.

Measure Correctly

How to Take Your Measurements

Use a soft measuring tape and keep the tape comfortably against your body without pulling it too tightly.

01

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the tape level across your back.

02

Waist

Measure around your natural waist without pulling the tape tightly.

03

Hip

Measure around the fullest part of your hips, keeping the measuring tape horizontal.

04

Shoulder

Measure from one shoulder point to the other across the back.

05

Sleeve

If required for your garment, measure from the shoulder point to your preferred sleeve length.

06

Length

For suits and dresses, measure from the shoulder down to your preferred finished garment length.

Before You Order

A Few Things to Keep in Mind

Measure Yourself First

Your current body measurements are more useful than relying only on the size you usually wear from another brand.

Product Details Matter

Different designs can have different cuts, silhouettes and construction. Always check the individual product information where available.

Please Remember

This guide is intended as a general sizing reference. Fit can vary depending on fabric, design, construction, tailoring and personal preference. If you are unsure between sizes, please contact ShriVatsaDarbar before ordering.
